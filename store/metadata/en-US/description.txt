Plate is a minimal gym tracker for people who lift. Log your sets fast, know exactly what to lift next, and see your progress build up. It works completely offline: no account, no ads, no cloud. Your data never leaves your iPhone.

LOG WORKOUTS FAST
• Start an empty workout or one of your routines
• Weight and reps per set, with warm-up, drop and failure sets
• Last session's numbers shown beside every set, one tap to copy
• Automatic rest timer after each set
• Live duration, volume and set count
• An unfinished workout survives closing the app

PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD, BUILT IN
• Set a rep range per exercise, for example 6 to 10
• Hit the top of the range on every set and Plate suggests more weight next time, otherwise one more rep
• Your next target is shown on every exercise
• Choose your own weight step, in kg or lb

ROUTINES
• Build routines with sets and rep ranges
• Start from Push / Pull / Legs or your own plan
• Repeat any past workout, or save it as a routine

750+ EXERCISES WITH PHOTOS
• Search and filter by muscle
• Two-frame photos for each exercise so you can check your form
• Step-by-step instructions
• Add your own custom exercises, including bodyweight and timed ones

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Charts for heaviest weight, estimated 1RM and volume per exercise
• Personal record flags on the sets where you set them
• Workouts and volume per week, sets per muscle, weekly goal and streak
• Body weight log

YOURS TO KEEP
• Everything is stored on your device
• Export a full backup and import it on a new phone
• Kilograms or pounds

Plate is a training log, not medical advice. Talk to a professional before starting a new exercise program.
