# Plate Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-17_

Plate does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- **Everything stays on your device.** Workouts, routines, body weight and settings are stored locally on your iPhone.
- **No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** Plate contains no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests.
- **Backups are yours.** If you use "Export backup", the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it.
- **Deleting the app deletes the data** (unless you made a backup). "Delete all data" in settings removes everything without deleting the app.

Plate is a training log and does not give medical advice.
